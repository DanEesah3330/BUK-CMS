Fast & Efficient Care

Seamless workflow from Reception to Nurse Triage, Doctor Consultation, Lab Tests, X-ray, and Pharmacy dispensing.

How a visit works

1

Register

Find the student by matric number and open the file.

2

Triage

The nurse records vital signs. Emergencies go first.

3

Consultation

The doctor sees the file, then requests a test or prescribes.

4

Pharmacy or Diagnostics

Results go back to the doctor. Drugs are dispensed and stock updates.
